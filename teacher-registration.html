Volunteer Teacher Recruitment | Roshd Academy

Roshd Academy invites skilled and responsible teachers to join us in providing free online classes for girls.

Volunteer Program

  • Duration: 2 months
  • Teaching: Fully online and voluntary
  • No fees are charged from students.
  • No financial payment is provided to volunteer teachers.
  • A certificate of collaboration will be provided after successful completion.

Recognition of Outstanding Volunteer Teachers

Teachers who demonstrate effective teaching, regularity, responsibility, and outstanding performance during the program may be recognized as outstanding volunteer teachers of Roshd Academy.

With their consent, their name, photograph, and a short introduction may be published on the official Roshd Academy website.
